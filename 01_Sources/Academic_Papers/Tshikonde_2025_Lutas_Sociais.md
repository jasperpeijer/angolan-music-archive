# Lutas Sociais em Angola no Contexto Contemporâneo (2011-2023)

## 1. Entities Linked & Key Figures Mentioned
### Political & Social Movements
*   **Movimento Revú:** A youth-led activist movement that emerged around 2011, inspired by the Arab Spring, focusing on demands for democracy, government accountability, and an end to the MPLA's long-standing rule.
*   **Movimento Hip-Hop:** A cultural and political resistance movement that served as a catalyst for youth political awareness in Angola, offering critical reflections on social challenges and opposing state hegemony.
*   **Ondjango Feminista:** A feminist collective established to promote women's and girls' human rights, gender equality, and combat domestic violence.
*   **Movimento Cívico Mudei:** A coalition of civil society organizations focused on electoral transparency and civic observation, particularly active during the 2022 elections.

### Key Figures (Activists, Musicians & Politicians)
*   **Luaty Beirão (Ikonoklasta / Brigadeiro Mata Frakuzx):** A prominent rapper and activist who played a key role in the early protests against José Eduardo dos Santos and was one of the "15+2" detainees.
*   **MCK:** A rapper known for his socially conscious lyrics ("rap de intervenção"), criticizing political arrogance and social exclusion.
*   **José Eduardo dos Santos:** Former President of Angola (1979-2017) whose long tenure and authoritarian governance were major targets of the Movimento Revú.
*   **João Lourenço:** President of Angola, whose initial promises of reform ("milagre econômico") were later overshadowed by continued authoritarian practices and repression of protests.
*   **Nito Alves (1945-1977):** A historical MPLA figure and leader of the "Poder Popular," whose dissidence led to the tragic events of May 27, 1977, a recurring reference in modern Angolan protests.

## 2. Conceptual Glossary: Resistance & Culture
*   **Bajú vs. Revú:** A binary political classification in Angolan popular culture. "Revú" (from revolutionary) denotes critics or dissidents of the MPLA regime, while "Bajú" (from *bajulador* or flatterer) refers to opportunistic supporters or sycophants of the government.
*   **Conscious Rap (Rap de Intervenção):** A subgenre of hip-hop characterized by its political, pedagogical, and social critique components, heavily utilized by Angolan youth to articulate political dissent.
*   **Kuduro:** An urban Angolan music and dance style. While often co-opted by the state, it is also used by marginalized youth (sometimes using the "Xtru" slang) to denounce police brutality and social injustice in the *musseques*.

## 3. Extracted Context & Clipped Texts

### A. The Role of Hip-Hop and Kuduro in Political Resistance
> *"o activismo, a dissidência e a crítica política na Angola do século XXI não podem ser entendidos sem referência à história do movimento Hip-Hop e rap no país, já que foi do seio desse universo que apareceram vários protagonistas do movimento activista..."*

*   **Data Extracted:** Hip-Hop in Angola is not just a musical genre but a foundational platform for 21st-century activism. The "conscious rap" movement provided the vocabulary and the protagonists for political dissent. While the state attempted to co-opt genres like Kuduro and promote commercial rap (e.g., the group SSP), underground rap remained a space of resistance against social inequality and political repression. Banda Movimento is not explicitly analyzed in this specific context of youth resistance, as the focus is heavily on Hip-Hop and Kuduro as the primary vehicles for the "Geração da Mudança" (Generation of Change).

### B. The 15+2 Case and State Repression
> *"O caso '15+2' tornou-se um marco emblemático da repressão política em Angola, destacando a intolerância do regime a manifestações e dissidências. Em junho de 2015, as autoridades angolanas detiveram 15 jovens ativistas acusados de conspirarem para um suposto golpe de Estado."*

*   **Data Extracted:** In 2015, 15 young activists (later joined by two women, hence "15+2") were arrested while discussing Gene Sharp's theories on non-violent resistance. The state framed this as a coup attempt. The ensuing trials and incarcerations (including Luaty Beirão's 36-day hunger strike) drew massive international condemnation from the UN, the European Parliament, and global human rights organizations, inadvertently strengthening civil society networks in Angola.

### C. The Illusion of Reform under João Lourenço
> *"Apesar de algumas medidas iniciais que pareciam promissoras, como a liberação de protestos e um discurso mais conciliatório, a dinâmica política em Angola voltou rapidamente a se concentrar em um modelo autoritário."*

*   **Data Extracted:** The transition from José Eduardo dos Santos to João Lourenço initially brought hope for democratization and anti-corruption reforms. However, this "abertura política" (political opening) was ephemeral. The state quickly reverted to authoritarian practices, utilizing new legal frameworks (like the Law on Crimes of Vandalism of Public Goods) and violent police crackdowns to severely restrict the right to protest, leading to the deaths and arbitrary detentions of several activists between 2020 and 2023.
