# [[Bangão]]

> **Core Summary:** 
---

## 1. Biography
Day Doy was born Adriano Francisco João in 1965 in the region of Ícolo e Bengo, located in the Luanda province of Angola (Now province Icolo e Bengo). His artistic journey began in the Golf neighborhood of Luanda, where he started playing and experimenting with music alongside friends and contemporaries. During his early years, he faced significant socio-economic difficulties; the high cost of living forced him to sing and play music in public spaces just to financially support himself.

Day Doy specializes in Semba music, with a strong focus on interpreting his songs in the Kimbundu language. At a time when there was a notable lack of Semba compositions in Kimbundu, his style sparked great interest among the Angolan public, leading to increased bookings for shows and cultural events. He is also noted for being part of the broader Kizomba movement, blending traditional Angolan heritage with contemporary appeal.

His major career breakthrough came through a connection with the late, legendary Angolan musician [[Teta Lando]]. Teta Lando helped facilitate a recording opportunity for Day Doy and his colleagues. Together, they formed an experimental trio and recorded the work _KAMAKA_ between 1995 and 1996. This project was highly successful, earning them the "Prémio Revelação" (Revelation Award) presented by Rádio Luanda.
