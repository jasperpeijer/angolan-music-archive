# [[Dom Caetano]]

> **Core Summary:** 
---

## 1. Biography
Caetano Domingos António, more commonly known as Dom Caetano, was born on April 25, 1958, in Bairro Popular, located in the province of Luanda. During his youth, between 1973 and 1975, he lived in the municipalities of Cazenga and Sambizanga.

Before establishing himself fully as a musician, Caetano pursued technical education and worked in the transport logistics sector. At the age of 18, in 1976, he traveled to Cuba to complete a course as a Medium Transport Technician. He returned to Angola in 1981, taking on the role of an engineering advisor for a public transport company. From 1983 to 1985, he served as the head of transport for the companies Emproe and Panga-Panga. His expertise in this field is notable; he is considered a specialist in traffic and transport economics, occasionally speaking on public infrastructure issues, such as advocating for better public transportation management in Luanda.

Following his work in transport, he studied journalism and subsequently worked at the Agência Angola Press (ANGOP).

### Musical Roots and Band Affiliations
Caetano's musical journey began early. In 1972, at just 16 years old, he took his first steps in singing. His stage debut occurred in 1973, and during the early 1970s in Sambizanga, he formed a group with friends called "The Seven Boys".

His career is deeply intertwined with several historic Angolan musical groups. He is prominently recognized as a second-generation artist of the iconic band [[Jovens do Prenda]], particularly active with them during the 1980s and 90s. In 1987, after two years of deep involvement with Jovens do Prenda, the demands of his musical career forced him to leave journalism behind completely. He also contributed his vocals to other notable groups, though his work with Jovens do Prenda remains a defining element of his musical identity, and he continues to perform with them in modern retrospectives like the "Muzonguê da Tradição".

### Solo Career
As a solo artist, Dom Caetano recorded his first album, [[Adão E Eva]], in Lisbon. This debut was highly successful, featuring major hit tracks such as [[Uejia Ki Usokana]] and [[Semba Dilema]]. He is also the author of heavily recognized Angolan anthems such as [[Som Angolano]].

### Cultural Advocacy & Copyright Leadership
Dom Caetano is known as a defender of traditional Angolan culture. He has been vocal about the need for quality control in modern music, publicly suggesting the creation of evaluation commissions to correct "musical trash" and preserve artistic standards ([[Angola24Horas_2018_Dom_Caetano_Contra|Angola24Horas, 2018]]). Furthermore, he has emphasized the urgent need for published biographical works to document the lives of key figures in national culture.

A recognized advocate for artists' rights, Dom Caetano was appointed as the General Director of the Associação Única de Direitos de Autor e Conexos (AUDAC) in early 2026, succeeding Lucioval Gama. He attributed this appointment to his long-standing, uncompromising defense of copyright issues. (Note: This transition was accompanied by legal friction, with Gama filing a criminal suit against Caetano over public statements, which Caetano stated he was prepared to contest in court) ([[Zango_2026_Dom_Caetano_Novo_Director|Zango, 2026]]).

## 3. Network & Collaborators
*   **Bands / Collectives:**
	* **[[The Seven Boys (Os Sete Amigos)]]:** Formed in the early 1970s in Sambizanga, this was his first musical group during his formative years.
	* **[[Conjunto Astros]]:** The band that backed him during his official stage debut in 1973.
	* **[[Jovens do Prenda]]:** One of Angola's most iconic musical ensembles. Caetano became a core second-generation vocalist for them in the 1980s, eventually leaving his journalism career to sing with them full-time.
	* **[[Banda Movimento]]:** A major collective that actively backed him for live performances and studio work later in his career, particularly during traditional retrospective concerts.
*   **Key Collaborators:**
	* **[[Zeca Sá]]:** Maintained a massive 35-year duet partnership ("Dom Caetano e Zeca Sá"). They released joint projects well into their later careers, including the 2018 retrospective album _Memórias 35 Anos_.
	* **[[Augusto Chacaya]] & [[Calabeto]]:** Fellow legendary _semba_ vocalists. Caetano frequently shares the stage with these contemporaries for traditional music revival events like the _Muzonguê da Tradição_.
