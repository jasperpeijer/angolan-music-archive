# [[Os Jovens do Prenda]]

> **Core Summary:** Formed in Luanda in 1968, Os Jovens do Prenda is a foundational Angolan ensemble celebrated for their distinct fusion of local Semba and Rumba rhythms. Serving as a "true school" for generations of artists, the group has utilized a massive, rotating roster of talent to maintain a 50-plus-year legacy at the forefront of Angolan music.

---

## 1. Biography
Os Jovens do Prenda emerged in Luanda in 1968 (some sources cite 1965), originally performing under the name _Jovens do Catambor_. That same year, they briefly changed their name to _Jovens da Maianga_ before officially adopting _Os Jovens do Prenda_ in 1969. The final name was suggested by local businessman Manguxi, owner of Salão Braguês, as a tribute to the historic Prenda neighborhood where the musicians lived.

Their signature sound was a dynamic fusion of local Angolan rhythms (like Semba) and international styles, heavily influenced by the guitar work of Congolese legend Dr. Nico. The band experienced a massive initial run from 1968 until 1974. Following a hiatus, they mounted a comeback in 1981 and have maintained an active presence ever since. Because of the sheer volume of musicians who passed through their ranks, foundational vocalist Luís Neto famously described the band not just as a musical group, but as "a true school" for Angolan artists.

### International Recognition & The 1990s
While already foundational figures in Angolan music, Os Jovens do Prenda achieved significant European recognition in the early 1990s. The band traveled to Germany to perform at the renowned Heimatklänge world music festival in Berlin. Their energetic Tempodrom performance was recorded live and released in 1993 by Piranha Records as the album _Berlin Festa!_. This live LP effectively exported their signature, complex guitar interplay to an international audience, proving that their deeply local sound could command global festival stages.

# Network & Collaborators
Because of their RNA residency, their network includes the absolute elite of Angolan music. They served as the primary backing band for:
- **Manguxi:** The businessman and owner of Salão Braguês who actively promoted the band in their infancy and gave them their definitive name.
- **Dr. Nico:** The renowned Congolese guitarist whose style was cited as a primary foundation and influence for the band's instrumental arrangements.
- **[[Os Kiezos]]:** Widely regarded as their direct musical peers. Historical archives note that Os Kiezos and Os Jovens do Prenda were considered the two greatest Angolan music groups through the 1990s, frequently sharing stages (such as the Kilamba cultural centre) to perform for massive crowds.
