# [[Beto de Almeida]]

> **Core Summary:** Beto de Almeida (1976–2013) was a celebrated Angolan singer and composer who, alongside his brother Moniz, formed the acclaimed duo [[Irmãos Almeida]]. Renowned for his distinct vocal style and lasting hits, he remains a pillar of modern Angolan popular music.

---

## 1. Biography
Beto D'Almeida emerged in the Angolan music scene in the late 1980s. Known affectionately as a _puto_ (kid) with a growing reputation in the southern region of Angola, he initially performed under the stage name **Beto Viola** in Lubango. During this period, his artistic work was heavily focused on children's music. His early talent was showcased to cultural promoters during a regional show called _Café com Leite_, organized in Lubango by the Núcleo dos Amigos da Infância (NAI).

### Irmãos Almeida & Solo Success
By 1989, Beto transitioned into mainstream Angolan popular music, entering the studio with his brother [[Moniz D'Almeida]] to record collaborative tracks such as "Eu espero", "Vou comer então aonde", and "Sozinho".

During this era, Beto released the solo hit "Cara de Pau". Concurrently, Moniz released "Tio Zé", a track that featured Beto providing vocal support before he was officially known to the wider public. Following their parallel successes, they officially merged to form [[Irmãos Almeida]], releasing their debut joint album, _Kimbanda_, in 1996. Throughout his career, Beto was known for his distinct vocal signature (sometimes referred to by peers as the "selo vocal de Tchuma") and his ability to seamlessly adapt to new musical trends.

### Death
Beto de Almeida passed away in the early hours of Sunday, October 6, 2013, in Windhoek, Namibia. Approximately a month prior, he had suffered a severe fall at a hotel in Lubango following a hypertensive episode (though some sources state it happened while performing). The fall resulted in a traumatic brain injury and paralysis on the left side of his brain. He was medically evacuated from Huíla to Namibia, where he remained hospitalized until his passing. His death was deeply mourned as a massive loss for Angolan culture.

## 3. Network & Collaborators
* **Moniz de Almeida:** His older brother and primary artistic partner. Together, they formed the renowned Kizomba duo [[Irmãos Almeida]], recording pivotal collaborative albums such as _Kimbanda_, _Pico_, _Almeizy_, _Invisível_, and _The Best+5_, and sharing foundational credits on historic tracks like _Tio Zé_.
* **Adão Filipe:** The promoter, radio personality, and current director of Rádio Benguela who worked closely with Beto de Almeida as a key force behind his early professional projection and entry into the Angolan music market.
* **Presilha & [[Livongh]]:** Contemporary artists who frequently partnered with Beto de Almeida on various music productions, collaborating closely with the distinct vocal style widely recognized in the industry.
* **Salú Gonçalves & Hirondino Garcia:** Cultural promoters and radio directors associated with early developmental milestones, including the NAI (_Núcleo dos Amigos da Infância_) _Café com Leite_ show in Lubango where Beto first gained widespread regional attention as a young performer (_Beto Viola_).
* **Hito Capitalista:** An event promoter and cultural figure who collaborated and crossed paths with Beto on regional projects, including the production and video-clip recording of his track _Balumuka_ and performances during the city celebrations of Huambo.
