# [[Eddy Tussa]]

> **Core Summary:** Eddy Tussa is one of the biggest semba artists, of this century. Originally a rap artist, he transitioned into semba music. He is known for making famous hits such as [[Vou Ficar Fininho]] and for remaking old songs such as [[Pequenina_Boano_da_Silva|Pequenina]].

---

## 1. Biography
Conceição Bartolomeu António, better known as [[Eddy Tussa]], was born in Luanda on March 29, 1977. He was born in the Cazenga municipality, on Sina 18 Rua do Comércio, the son of parents from Malanje named Bartolomeu António and Suzana Manuel Paixão. According to Eddy Tussa, if he were not a musician, his dream would have been to be the president of the country. He lived in Cazenga until he was 14, moving to Avenida Brasil in Luanda and later to the Cassenda neighborhood, where he spent his youth before emigrating to Lisbon. He attended primary school at the "Escola Grande do Cazenga" and secondary school at Puniv.

### Career
Eddy Tussa is one of the greatest current artists in Angola's traditional urban music, Semba. Eddy Tussa emerged in the music market as a member of the rap group Warrant-B. Semba, however, remains his musical essence. He formed two companies in the fields of entertainment, construction, hospitality and tourism, transport, etc., one of which is LS HUMOR FACTORY with (Pedro Nzagi) and the other KASSEMBELE TUSSA Ltd.

In 2010, he released his first solo album titled [[Izenu Mu Tale]]. In 2012, he released the album [[Grandes Mundos]]. Later, in 2015, he released the album [[Kassembele]]. Eddy Tussa is known for compositions such as [[Muimbo Uami]], [[Retrato]], [[Diala Diame]], [[Mama Koleno]], [[Homenagem]], [[Kassembele (Song)|Kassembele]], [[Amor Mwangole]], [[Vou Ficar Fininho]], and [[Nzenze]], among others, and is one of the most remarkable musicians in Angolan music over the last 20 years. A member of the production company Karioka Entretenimento, he was also a member of Ls Produções, eventually forming a company with (Pedro Nzagi) in the humor sector, LS HUMOR FACTORY.

Represented by Paula Summer, the musician has performed numerous shows across Angola's 18 provinces and has also performed on international stages in Portugal, Belgium, France, the Netherlands, Germany, and the United States. He has been the traveling ambassador for the "I Love Semba" brand, which has been drawing crowds to dance floors and stages across Europe.

### Personal Life
The singer is married to Ana Epifánia Francisco António, with whom he has four children: Nuria, Lucas, Alika, and Stewart.
