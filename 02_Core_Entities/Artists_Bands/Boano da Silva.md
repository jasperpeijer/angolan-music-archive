# [[Boano da Silva]]

> **Core Summary:** Boano António Manuel da Silva is/was a relatively unknown artist. He is primarily known for the song Pequenina, which was remade in 2015 by [[Eddy Tussa]].

---

## 1. Biography
Boano António Manuel da Silva was born in the Province of Luanda on March 9, 1937. He identified himself in the Angolan musical context by the stage name **Boano da Silva**, having previously recorded several songs he authored for other labels, which contributed in their own way to the listener's taste.

As a worker and a conscious citizen, Boano da Silva reflects that as the revolutionary process advances in our country, he must necessarily contribute to National Reconstruction. He is enrolled in the O.D.P. (Organization of People's Defense), holding specific responsibilities.

For him, singing is the most viable way to convey his feelings regarding his militancy, which he has held since joining the organization, to the combatant comrades of the O.D.P. in general and to the Angolan People. He appeals especially to O.D.P. combatants in general to increase their level of political consciousness through study; and to generalize vigilance in factories, in workplaces, and in the fields, fighting reaction, because the struggle still continues." 

_Information written on the back cover of Semba Dipanda. Nothing more to add._

We leave you here with "Semba Dipanda," the A-side of this single, one of the songs that cements the name of Boano da Silva as a major figure in Angolan music, not forgetting his fundamental role with the **Dimba Dya Ngola**, another elementary name in Angolan polyphonic culture and soul. Note the spectacular and engaging guitars, which mark identifying traits of Central African polyphony. Boano da Silva's voice, sublime and expansive as always, traces the narrative contours of the lyrics through gaps and words fashioned in a single language: that of urgency. The cover is absolutely stunning, one of the simplest and most beautiful ever made by N'Gola.
