# [[Adão Gonçalves]] / [[Jivago]]

> **Core Summary:** A definitive voice of the 1980s Semba transition, Jivago mastered the *crónica social*—chronicling the everyday humor, romance, and moral tensions of Luanda's musseques during the height of the Angolan civil war. 

---

## 1. Biography

### Origins & Formative Years
* Adão Gonçalves was born in the province of Luanda in 1954. 
* He officially entered the music scene far from the capital, joining the group **Bentiaba Show** in the southern coastal city of Namibe in 1977. 

### Peak Era & Political Context
* In 1983, he joined the band **Fenomenal**, a critical move that resulted in the recording of his smash hit [[Avó Tete]]. 
* After leaving Fenomenal, he embarked on a successful solo career. In 1989, he recorded his other massive hit, [[Ramiro]], live at the auditorium of the Rádio Nacional de Angola (RNA).
* Passed away on October 1, 2020, at Hospital do Prenda in Luanda due to a hypertensive crisis and pulmonary complications. His death occurred on International Music Day, just weeks after Angola lost other musical titans like Waldemar Bastos and Carlos Burity.
