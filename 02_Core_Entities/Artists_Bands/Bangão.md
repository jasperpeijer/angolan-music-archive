# [[Bangão]]

> **Core Summary:** Bangão was a master of _semba_ whose music served as the conscience of Luanda, blending deep-rooted tradition with the urban life of Luanda. He is remembered as a defiant chronicler of the Angolan experience, whose art captured both the profound suffering and the enduring resilience of his community.

---

## 1. Biography
Bernardo Jorge Martins Correia, known to the world as Bangão, was one of the most respected and influential figures in Angolan music. Born on September 27, 1962, in the Bairro Brás area of Sambizanga, Luanda, he grew up immersed in the vibrant cultural life of the city’s historic neighborhoods. This upbringing became the cornerstone of his artistry, allowing him to capture the spirit of Luandan life in his lyrics and melodies.

Bangão’s musical journey began in the late 1970s. He started performing publicly in 1978 with the group Gingas Kakulo Kalunga, following earlier stints with groups like Tradição and Processo de África. However, it was his evolution as a songwriter and performer that cemented his status as a master of _semba_. His breakthrough came in 1996 when he won the prestigious Liceu Vieira Dias prize for his iconic song "Kibuikila," a powerful social critique performed with Banda Movimento. He continued to gain national acclaim in 1999, joining Banda Movimento as a vocalist and winning the first edition of the Semba de Ouro competition with his hit "Kangila."

Throughout his life, Bangão was celebrated not just as a singer, but as a chronicler of the Angolan experience. His later albums, _Sembele_ and _Cuidado_, are recognized as essential works that preserved the soul of Angolan music. Following a long fight against cancer, he passed away on May 18, 2015, in Pretoria, South Africa, at the age of 52. He is remembered by his peers and the public as one of the greatest exponents of _semba_, a musician whose voice served as the conscience of his community.
