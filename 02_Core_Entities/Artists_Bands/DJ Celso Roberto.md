# [[DJ Celso Roberto]]

> **Core Summary:** Celso Roberto Ferreira Carlos is a prominent Angolan actor, former model, and DJ, widely recognized for his leading television roles in both Angola and Portugal. Drawing on his early roots as a radio sound engineer and club DJ, he transitioned into music production in 2017 with the release of _Karavana da Saudade_, an ambitious album dedicated to reimagining classic Angolan tracks for modern dancefloors.

---

## 1. Biography
Celso Roberto was born in the municipality of Cubal in Angola's Benguela province. Long before his television fame, his professional life began in the music industry, where he worked from a very young age as a radio sound engineer and an active club DJ. Concurrently, he built a successful career in fashion, working as a model for runways, television commercials, and billboards between 1999 and 2012.

His major breakthrough came in the acting world. He made his television debut in 2001 in _Vidas Ocultas_, an Angolan miniseries based on a novel by Pepetela. In 2012, he achieved international recognition by starring as Kiluanji Voss in the massively successful, Emmy-nominated Angolan telenovela _Windeck_. This success allowed him to expand his acting career into Portugal, where he secured prominent roles in major Portuguese TVI soap operas, including _A Única Mulher_ (2015) and _A Impostora_ (2018).

Despite his acting success, Celso Roberto never abandoned his musical roots. In late 2017, he officially debuted as a singer and producer under the moniker DJ Celso Roberto. His passion project, _Karavana da Saudade_, was aimed at reviving the classic, sometimes forgotten, tracks he used to spin during his days as an active DJ. Rather than using electronic programming, he insisted on completely re-recording the tracks with live instrumentation to give them a fresh, highly danceable energy.

## 3. Network & Collaborators
* **Music Collaborators (Karavana da Saudade):** To ensure the authenticity of his 2017 album, he collaborated with a massive roster of legendary Angolan vocalists, including Augusto Chakaya, Eddy Tussa, Carlos Burity, Gersy Pegado, Dom Caetano, and Dina Santos.
* **Television & Acting Network:** He frequently shared the screen with prominent Angolan and Portuguese actors, including Micaela Reis, Fredy Costa, Alexandra Lencastre, and Diogo Infante during his runs on _Windeck_ and TVI productions.
