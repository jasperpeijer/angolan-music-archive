# [[Augusto Chakaya]]

> **Core Summary:** Augusto Chakaya was a charismatic lead vocalist for the legendary Angolan ensemble [[Os Jovens do Prenda]], cementing his legacy during the band's 1980s and 1990s eras. Renowned as a guardian of the Kimbundu language and Angolan cultural identity, he used Semba to narrate the everyday struggles, tragedies, and wisdom of the people until his passing in 2024.

---

## 1. Biography
Born on March 18, Augusto Chakaya rose to national prominence in the 1980s and 1990s as a primary voice for [[Os Jovens do Prenda]]. While the band functioned as a massive school of rotating musicians, Chakaya distinguished himself not just as a singer, but as a true interpreter of the Angolan soul.

His compositions were deeply rooted in the Kimbundu language and often tackled heavy societal and personal themes. He sang about the destructive realities of alcoholism, the daily struggles of the working class, and profound personal grief, most notably in a heartbreaking musical tribute to his late daughter, Sandra.

Cultural researchers and government officials alike celebrated him as a pillar of cultural resistance. By preserving traditional narratives and language through the post-independence era, Chakaya helped ensure that Semba wasn't just entertainment, but a vital tool for education and historical preservation. He remained active in the music scene, notably collaborating on modern projects like DJ Celso Roberto's 2017 album, until he passed away from illness in Luanda in September 2024.

## 3. Network & Collaborators
* **Os Jovens do Prenda:** He was a defining vocalist for their later eras, performing alongside foundational members and later musicians.
* **DJ Celso Roberto:** Collaborated on the 2017 album _Karavana da Saudade_ to record a modernized version of [[Malamba Mami]].
* **Peers & Contemporaries:** Frequently shared the stage with other Angolan music legends like Tony do Fumo, Lulas da Paixão, and Massano Júnior at cultural events in Luanda.
