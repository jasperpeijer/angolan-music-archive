# Parece Que Está Na Moda by [[Dino]]

> **Thematic Core:** [[Parece Que Está Na Moda]] is a song about how women these days only go for married men and not for single guys.

---

## 1. Context & Linguistic Significance
[[Parece Que Está Na Moda]] is a humorous look at a frustrating dating trend: the narrator realizes that young women are suddenly ignoring single guys and going straight for married men. According to the song, being married is almost like a status symbol or a "business card" because the forbidden nature of it makes the guy more desirable. It's a playful but sharp critique of infidelity, status-chasing, and how people always seem to want what they aren't supposed to have.
