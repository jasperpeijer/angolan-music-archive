# Sengola by [[Lina Alexandre]]

> **Thematic Core:** *Sengola* is an incisive _kizomba_ track in Kikongo addressing matrimonial neglect, domestic abuse, and the destructive consequences of alcoholism. Sung from the perspective of an elder or in-law, the track demands that a husband's shameful conduct be dragged into the public light, warning that customary law and family mediators will not allow him to mistreat his bride without consequence.

---

## 1. Context & Linguistic Significance
The title *Sengola* is an imperative verb meaning to "uncover," "reveal," or "expose" that which is hidden. Rooted in Kongo customary marriage practices, the song centers on the husband's moral and legal duty to nourish and protect a young bride entrusted to his household. 

Instead of allowing domestic strife to stay behind closed doors, Lina Alexandre utilizes the traditional Kikongo vocabulary of elder reprimand, customary court fines, and proverbial wisdom to turn a private marital dispute into a communal moral trial.
