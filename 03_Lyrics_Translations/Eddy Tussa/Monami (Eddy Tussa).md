# Monami by [[Eddy Tussa]]

> **Thematic Core:** *Monami* by Tony de Fumo is a heartfelt dialogue from an overwhelmed father trying to comfort his crying child by reminding them that he has provided everything they could ever want. He expresses a deep love and a desire to pass down his profession of singing, while gently pleading for a moment of peace because his mind is heavily burdened with personal worries.

---

## 1. Context & Linguistic Significance
This old song was originally recorded by [[Tony do Fumo]], backed by [[Os Kiezos]]. It was later interpreted by [[Eddy Tussa]], who keeps the roots of the song, while giving it a more modern feel. The text stays almost entirely the same, with only minor changes, such as a changed line, change of the order of lines, and small changes in pronounciation.

## 5. Notes
* The song, originally sung by [[Tony do Fumo]], was originally released in the 70s as part of the *Nossa Senhora Do Monte / Monami* vinyl of [[Os Kiezos]]. It was later rereleased as part of [[Memorias]], a compilation album of [[Tony do Fumo]]. [[Eddy Tussa]] then later recorded a newer version of it.
